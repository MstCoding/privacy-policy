01

About This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Steel Front: Industrial Defense (the “App”). It describes the App’s actual information practices as of the effective date above. The App is an offline, single-player game and its core features do not require an account or a network connection.

02

Information the App Handles

The App does not collect personal or sensitive information from gameplay. It does not ask for your name, phone number, address, identity document, payment details, or account credentials.

  • Local game data: The App stores campaign progress such as unlocked missions, completed missions, difficulty results, and tactical item inventory. It can also store the selected language, visual style, onboarding status, and a paused-battle snapshot. This information remains in the local app data area and is not sent to a server by the App.
  • Optional email communication: If you email the contact address in this policy, we receive your email address, message, and anything else you choose to include. This information is used to respond to your request.
  • No sensitive data sources: The App does not access contacts, precise location, photos or files, camera, microphone, health information, or advertising identifiers.
03

How Information Is Used

Local game data is used only to restore your campaign, settings, and paused session. Information sent by email is used only to answer questions, provide support, investigate a report, or respond to a privacy request. The App does not use personal information for advertising, behavioral profiling, or unrelated marketing.

04

Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Local game data is retained on your device or in the local storage area of the environment where you play until you remove it. You can use the App’s “Reset Campaign Progress” control to delete campaign progress. To remove all locally saved game information, including settings and a paused session, use the app-data management controls provided by your environment or remove the App.

We retain email correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, provide support, resolve a related issue, or meet a legal obligation. The email service provider may retain messages under its own privacy terms.

05

Sharing and Service Providers

The App does not sell, rent, or trade personal information. It does not transmit local game data to third parties. When you contact us by email, an email service provider processes the message to deliver and store it. Any provider handling information on our behalf is expected to use it only to provide the relevant service and protect it as required by applicable law. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect users, property, or service security.

06

Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

The current version of the App does not serve advertisements, use behavior analytics, create advertising profiles, use cross-service tracking, or include a social login system. It also does not intentionally set tracking cookies. The App’s language selection on this page is handled in the current session and is not saved as a personal profile.

07

Permissions and Network Access

The App does not require access to personal data sources for its gameplay. Its bundled art and sound assets, game logic, and local save data are used locally. The current version has no account registration, cloud save, multiplayer service, remote telemetry, or in-app payment flow.

08

Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children and does not require a child to create an account or provide personal details. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has sent personal information to us by email, please contact us. We will review the request and delete the information where required by applicable law.

09

Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You control local game data through the reset and data-management options described in Section 4. For information included in an email, you may ask us to provide access to, correct, or delete that information by contacting us below. We will handle the request in accordance with applicable law. Removing local data does not delete an email that has already been sent; email deletion requests should be made directly to us.

10

Updates and Contact

If the App’s information practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date before or when the change takes effect. The App name and the contact address below identify the policy’s scope and privacy contact.

关于本政策

本隐私政策适用于《钢铁防线：工业守卫战》（以下简称“本应用”），说明截至上述生效日期本应用实际的信息处理方式。本应用是一款离线单人游戏，核心功能不要求账号或网络连接。

02

本应用处理的信息

本应用不会从游戏过程中收集个人信息或敏感信息，不要求您提供姓名、电话号码、地址、身份证件、支付信息或账号凭据。

  • 可选的邮件沟通：如果您向本政策中的联系邮箱发送邮件，我们会收到您的邮箱地址、邮件内容以及您主动附带的其他信息，仅用于回复您的请求。

03

信息的使用方式

本地游戏数据仅用于恢复您的战役、设置和暂停中的游戏。本应用通过邮件收到的信息仅用于回答问题、提供支持、调查反馈或回复隐私请求。本应用不会将个人信息用于广告、行为画像或无关营销。

04

存储、保留与删除

本地游戏数据会一直保存在您的设备或游玩环境的本地存储区域，直到您主动删除。您可以使用本应用中的“重置战役进度”控件删除战役进度。如需删除包括设置和暂停战场快照在内的全部本地游戏信息，请使用游玩环境提供的应用数据管理功能，或移除本应用。

我们仅在回复请求、提供支持、处理相关问题或履行法律义务所合理必要的期限内保留邮件往来。邮箱服务提供方可能依据其自身隐私条款保留邮件。

05

信息共享与服务提供方

本应用不会出售、出租或交易个人信息，也不会将本地游戏数据传输给第三方。当您通过邮件联系我们时，邮箱服务提供方会为传递和存储邮件而处理相关内容。代表我们处理信息的服务提供方应仅为提供相关服务而使用这些信息，并按照适用法律要求进行保护。在法律要求，或为保护用户、财产或服务安全所必需时，相关信息也可能被披露。

06

广告、分析与追踪

当前版本不会投放广告、使用行为分析工具、建立广告画像、进行跨服务追踪或提供社交登录，也不会主动设置追踪 Cookies。本页面的语言切换只在当前会话中生效，不会保存为个人资料。

07

权限与网络访问

本应用的游戏功能不需要访问个人数据来源。应用内置的美术和音频资源、游戏逻辑及本地存档均在本地使用。当前版本没有账号注册、云端存档、多人服务、远程运行数据上报或应用内支付流程。

08

未成年人隐私

本应用不会在明知的情况下收集未成年人个人信息，也不要求未成年人创建账号或提供个人资料。如果家长或监护人认为未成年人通过邮件向我们发送了个人信息，请联系我们。我们会核查请求，并在适用法律要求的范围内删除相关信息。

09

您的选择与隐私请求

您可以通过第四条所述的重置和数据管理选项控制本地游戏数据。对于邮件中包含的信息，您可以通过下方联系方式请求查询、更正或删除，我们会按照适用法律处理。删除本地数据不会删除已经发送的邮件；如需删除邮件内容，请直接向我们提出请求。

10

更新与联系我们
